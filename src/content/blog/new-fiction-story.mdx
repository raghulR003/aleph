## Opening

Start with an image. Something that grounds the reader immediately. A sound. A smell. A body position.

Don't explain too much yet. Let them wonder.

---

## The Turn

Something shifts here. A revelation. A arrival. A phone call. A body found.

Keep paragraphs short. Pulp fiction breathes in white space.

---

## The Drop

The ending should land with weight. Not necessarily a twist — sometimes a twist cheapens what came before. But a final line that echoes.

Something the reader carries with them.
