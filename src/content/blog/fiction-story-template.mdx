## Story setup

Use this when you want a standalone piece of fiction on the blog.

- keep `draft: true` while shaping the piece
- add a cover image only if it genuinely helps
- for fiction, strong scene breaks matter more than lots of headings
- if the story is multilingual, wrap the body in `<LanguageTabs>`

## Suggested structure

1. opening image or shock
2. scene turn
3. time jump or revelation
4. ending line with sting

<Callout type="tip" title="For pulp pieces">
  Keep paragraphs short, images vivid, and scene turns sharp.
</Callout>
